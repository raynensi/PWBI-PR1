# 🛒 SAMPLE SUPERSTORE
### 📊 Sales & Profit AnalysiS

## ✨ About the Project

What drives retail sales? Which products generate the most profit, and where can a business improve?

The **Sample Superstore Sales Analysis** project explores retail transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional trends, and shipping methods.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support smarter, data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 💰 Analyze total sales and profit performance.
- 🛍️ Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- 🌎 Identify high-performing and low-performing regions.
- 👥 Understand customer segment performance.
- 🚚 Analyze shipping methods and order patterns.
- 📅 Explore monthly and yearly sales trends.
- 🏷️ Investigate the relationship between discounts and profit.
- 📊 Present insights through charts and dashboards.
